# agu-quant 项目简介

## 中文

agu-quant 是一个 A 股研究工具，把行情、板块、条件选股、因子回测和模拟交易放在同一个本地界面里。单股分析会结合行情、新闻和基本面，调用语言模型整理成报告。

项目用 Python 开发，网页界面使用 Streamlit。可以先查看市场和板块，再按条件筛选股票，进一步检查因子或运行单股分析。模拟盘用于观察策略表现。

代码和启动说明：[ARETE-zzwl/agu-quant](https://github.com/ARETE-zzwl/agu-quant)。

界面保留了基础功能、赞赏激活和邮箱试用入口。模型服务与行情接口需要自行配置。项目仅供学习研究，不构成投资建议。

## English

agu-quant is a local research tool for Chinese A-shares. It brings market and sector views, stock screening, factor backtesting and paper trading into a Streamlit interface. Stock reports combine market data, news and fundamentals with language-model analysis.

Start with the market view, screen stocks by your own criteria, then inspect factors or run a stock report. The paper-trading module records simulated positions and trades.

Code and setup: [ARETE-zzwl/agu-quant](https://github.com/ARETE-zzwl/agu-quant).

The UI retains basic features, premium activation and email trial entry points. Configure model services and data access for your own environment. This project is for learning and research and does not provide investment advice.
